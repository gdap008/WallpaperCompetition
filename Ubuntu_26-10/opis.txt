Informations about files for Ubuntu 26.10 "Stonking Stingray" Wallpaper Competition.

Author: GrzesiekP (as gdap008)
Used Software:
  - Blender was used for main project and used to final render
  - Gnu Image Manipulation Program (GIMP) was used for concept art of stingray main shape.

Interesting fact about creation of this work
  I didn't have it in my plans to create this repository and the wallpaper for competition, 
  because I creating wallpapers for myself with my logo and my own concept art.
  Of course I use Ubuntu accent colors because I like it, but not in all my works i use them.
  Thing that convinced my to join the competition was a ending of my summer vacations, 
  because I wanted to used last moments of vacation.
  I was joined at last and I was happy with myself.
